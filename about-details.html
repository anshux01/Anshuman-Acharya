Hi, I'm Anshuman.

A Computer Science graduate building toward Data Science and AI/ML, with a strong foundation in software development, analytics and automation.

I graduated with a B.Tech in Computer Science & IT from Gandhi Institute for Technological Advancements (GITA), Bhubaneswar. My software-development background gave me a foundation in programming, databases and application development, and I am now focused on applying that foundation to Data Science and AI.

I completed the Professional Program in Data Science & AI at 360DigiTMG, Hyderabad, where I developed hands-on skills in Python, SQL, statistics, data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, visualization, machine learning and Power BI.

My project work has moved from traditional web applications toward data-driven and intelligent systems. This includes an AI-based road-marking detection system using computer vision, analytics projects, OCR automation, real-time applications and database-driven systems.

I also have experience with data annotation and quality-review workflows, including structured content classification and schema-based checking. That work strengthened my attention to consistency, validation and data quality.

I enjoy projects where technology solves a practical problem. My approach is usually to understand the problem first, prepare reliable data, build a useful solution, test it, and present the result clearly.
